HisaBook Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Application Name: HisaBook

1. Introduction

Welcome to HisaBook. HisaBook is a business management application designed to help users manage merchant information, customer records, financial accounts, debts, and transactions.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and financial information you provide through our application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored and shared, and what rights you have regarding your data.

By using HisaBook, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

HisaBook may collect and process the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information

  • Merchant and customer names.
  • Phone numbers.
  • Email addresses.
  • Account identification information.
  • User authentication information required to access the application.

B. Financial Information

  • Customer debt balances.
  • Merchant account balances.
  • Outstanding payments.
  • Financial transactions and account records.
  • Other financial information entered by users.

This information is collected to provide the application's financial and account management features.

C. Technical and Diagnostic Information

We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application stability and identify technical problems. This service may automatically collect:

  • Crash reports and error logs.
  • Stack traces.
  • Application state information related to crashes.
  • Device and application information, such as device model and operating system version.
  • Crashlytics installation identifiers.

This information helps us identify errors, improve application performance, and provide a more reliable experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To authenticate users and manage access to the application.
  • Business Management: To organize merchant and customer records.
  • Financial Management: To track debts, balances, payments, and financial transactions.
  • Application Functionality: To provide the application's main features and store relevant information.
  • Security: To help protect user accounts and maintain application security.
  • Crash Reporting: To identify, investigate, and fix technical errors using Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Service Improvement: To improve application stability and functionality.

We do not use financial records for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

HisaBook uses Firebase services provided by Google to support its backend infrastructure. These services include:

  • Firebase Authentication: Used to manage user authentication and account access.
  • Cloud Firestore / Firebase Database: Used to store and manage application information, including merchant, customer, and financial records.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash reports and technical diagnostic information.

We rely on Firebase's security infrastructure and implement appropriate security measures to help protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Data transmitted through Firebase services is protected using encrypted connections, such as HTTPS/TLS.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

HisaBook does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal or financial information to third parties.

We may share or transmit limited information to trusted service providers when necessary to operate the application.

These services include:

Google Firebase

  • Firebase Authentication processes authentication-related information.
  • Firebase Database services store and manage application records.
  • Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports and diagnostic information.

These services process information according to their respective privacy and security practices.

We do not share customer or merchant financial records with advertising companies.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal processes, or to protect the security and rights of users and the application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal and financial information for as long as necessary to provide the application's services and fulfill legitimate operational requirements.

Users may request the deletion of their accounts and associated personal information by contacting us through the contact information provided below.

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will take reasonable steps to delete the associated account information and records from our active systems, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Some information may remain temporarily in secure backups or be retained when required for legitimate legal, security, or compliance purposes. Such information will be deleted when it is no longer required.

Crash reports and diagnostic information are retained according to the retention settings and policies of Firebase Crashlytics.

7. User Rights

Depending on applicable privacy laws, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of their personal information and account.
  • Ask questions about how their information is collected and processed.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable limitations.

To exercise these rights, users can contact us using the contact details below.

8. Children's Privacy

HisaBook is a business management application and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application's features, services, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
